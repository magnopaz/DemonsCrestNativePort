===============================================================================
                       DEMON'S CREST (SNES)
                      ENDINGS & REQUIREMENTS GUIDE
===============================================================================

Demon's Crest features four distinct endings based on your overall completion 
percentage, the items you have collected, and whether you defeat the secret 
true final boss.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BAD ENDING (Short Ending / "The Coward")
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Completion Level: Low (Minimal items collected)
• Unlock Conditions:
  - Defeat Phalanx in Stage 5 after collecting only the bare minimum mandatory 
    crests/items.
  - Do NOT collect all Crests or Crest Fragments.
• Outcome:
  - Firebrand defeats Phalanx and claims the Crest of Heaven, but he lacks 
    full power. The realm falls into instability, and Firebrand vanishes into 
    obscurity without attaining true legendary status.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. NORMAL ENDING (Standard Ending)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Completion Level: Moderate (~50% to 99% item collection)
• Unlock Conditions:
  - Defeat Phalanx in Stage 5.
  - Collect most Crests, Talismans, and Health Extensions, but leave at least 
    one collectible item or Crest missing (do NOT reach 100% completion).
• Outcome:
  - Firebrand defeats Phalanx, unites the elemental Crests, and casts them into 
    the sea to prevent further conflict, ruling as the undisputed lord of the 
    Demon Realm.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. GOOD ENDING (Secret Boss Unlocked)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Completion Level: 100% Item Completion
• Unlock Conditions:
  1. Collect ALL 5 main Crests (Fire, Earth, Air, Water, Time).
  2. Collect ALL 5 Fire Crest Fragments / Weapon Shots (Buster, Tornado, Claw, 
     Dragon).
  3. Collect ALL 16 Life Extensions / Health Tanks.
  4. Collect ALL 5 Urns.
  5. Collect ALL 5 Vellums.
  6. Collect ALL 5 Talismans.
  7. Upon reaching 100% completion, enter the Overworld Map to automatically 
     receive the Crest of Heaven (Ultimate Form) and a special 100% completion 
     password.
  8. Enter the newly unlocked secret stage on the Overworld Map and DEFEAT the 
     true final boss: The Dark Demon (Ultimate Gargoyle form).
• Outcome:
  - Firebrand proves his supremacy by defeating the ultimate evil, cementing his 
    legend as the undisputed ruler of all demons.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SECRET / SPECIAL ENDING (Ultimate Completion Password)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Completion Level: True Master (Post-Dark Demon)
• Unlock Conditions:
  - Defeat the Dark Demon and view the Good Ending credits roll.
  - At the end of the credits, a special password (`QFFB KNST BDR2 SA22` or 
    `AR3S J42S G743 ML14` depending on regional version) is displayed.
  - Enter this password from the main menu to view a unique special epilogue 
    screen and full credits without having to replay the game.
