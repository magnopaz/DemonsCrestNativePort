                       DEMON'S CREST (SNES)
                   EVERY ITEM LOCATION GUIDE

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CRESTS & FIRE CREST WEAPONS (10 TOTAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Crest of Fire (Base):
  - Location: Starter item (held at the beginning of the game).

• Buster Fire Shot:
  - Location: Stage 1 (Colosseum / Graveyard)
  - Details: Rewarded upon defeating the boss Ovununu in the graveyard.

• Crest of Earth (Ground Gargoyle):
  - Location: Stage 1 (Cliff / Forest Pass)
  - Details: Rewarded upon defeating Arma in his 1st encounter.

• Tornado Fire Shot:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest)
  - Details: Rewarded upon defeating the Flame Lord boss.

• Crest of Air (Aerial Gargoyle):
  - Location: Stage 5 (Sky / Mountain Towers)
  - Details: Rewarded upon defeating Arma in his 2nd encounter.

• Crest of Water (Tidal Gargoyle):
  - Location: Stage 3 (Cathedral / Flooded Caves)
  - Details: Rewarded upon defeating Belth in the underground cavern.

• Claw Fire Shot:
  - Location: Stage 3 (Cathedral Upper Shaft)
  - Details: Rewarded upon defeating the boss Flier high up in the cathedral.

• Dragon Fire Shot:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest / Secret Pond Area)
  - Details: Defeat the secret underwater boss Holothurion (requires Water Crest 
    to dive into the forest lake).

• Crest of Time (Legendary Gargoyle):
  - Location: Stage 5 (Mountain Peak)
  - Details: Rewarded upon defeating Grewon the Wolf Boss at the mountain summit.

• Crest of Heaven (Ultimate Form):
  - Location: Overworld Map
  - Details: Unlocked automatically on the map after collecting 100% of all other 
    items (Crests, Fragments, Health Extensions, Urns, Vellums, and Talismans).

2. LIFE EXTENSIONS / HEALTH TANKS (16 TOTAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Life Extension #1:
  - Location: Stage 1 (Colosseum)
  - Details: Right after defeating Somulo (the dragon), headbutt the gargoyle 
    statue on the wall before exiting the area.

• Life Extension #2:
  - Location: Stage 1 (Colosseum)
  - Details: Re-visit after obtaining the Buster Shot. Break through the stone 
    block wall inside the opening room.

• Life Extension #3:
  - Location: Stage 1 (Colosseum)
  - Details: In the middle of the arena, drop down into a hidden shaft using 
    the Buster Shot or Ground Gargoyle to smash stone blocks.

• Life Extension #4:
  - Location: Stage 1 (Cliff Pass)
  - Details: In the climbing section, use Buster or Ground Gargoyle to break 
    a block wall near the top right ledge.

• Life Extension #5:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Town / Underground Catacombs)
  - Details: Inside the Catacombs, smash a weak stone wall at the bottom of 
    the second level with Buster / Ground Gargoyle.

• Life Extension #6:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest)
  - Details: Use the Tornado Shot or Aerial Gargoyle to reach a high tree branch 
    hidden behind leaves in the upper right quadrant.

• Life Extension #7:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest Reservoir / Submerged Ruins)
  - Details: Dive into the deep water pool using Tidal Gargoyle. Go through the 
    hidden cavern to find a urn containing the health tank.

• Life Extension #8:
  - Location: Stage 3 (Cathedral)
  - Details: Climb the spiked shaft using the Claw Shot to reach a high platform 
    alcove on the upper left side.

• Life Extension #9:
  - Location: Stage 3 (Flooded Caves)
  - Details: Use Tidal Gargoyle to swim into a narrow underwater cavern on 
    the bottom-left path.

• Life Extension #10:
  - Location: Stage 4 (Underground River)
  - Details: Swim against the strong current using Tidal Gargoyle to reach an 
    isolated stone ledge.

• Life Extension #11:
  - Location: Stage 4 (Catacombs Deep Room)
  - Details: Light all candles/torches in the dark room using the standard Fire 
    shot to spawn a jar containing the extension.

• Life Extension #12:
  - Location: Stage 5 (Mountain Pass)
  - Details: Break open a large ice/rock boulder with the Ground Gargoyle charge.

• Life Extension #13:
  - Location: Stage 5 (Sky Towers)
  - Details: Fly to the absolute top-left corner using Aerial Gargoyle outside 
    the windy tower section.

• Life Extension #14:
  - Location: Overworld Shop / Game Hall (Trio the Pago)
  - Details: Win the skull-matching mini-game on the highest difficulty setting.

• Life Extension #15:
  - Location: Stage 5 (Phalanx's Castle Exterior)
  - Details: Before entering the throne room, fly up to the castle bell tower with 
    Aerial Gargoyle and headbutt the giant bell 5 times.

• Life Extension #16:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest - Deep Burn Area)
  - Details: Re-visit after acquiring Ground Gargoyle; dash through a heavy boulder 
    wall in the burning section.

3. URNS (5 TOTAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Urn #1:
  - Location: Stage 1 (Cliff Pass)
  - Details: In the canyon section with floating platforms, drop down the pit 
    on the left side and go left into a hidden alcove.

• Urn #2:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Underground Catacombs)
  - Details: Use Buster to break a stone wall in the middle section of the catacombs.

• Urn #3:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest Pond)
  - Details: Dive underwater with Tidal Gargoyle at the third gap in the water 
    area and head right.

• Urn #4:
  - Location: Stage 3 (Cathedral)
  - Details: Inside the windy shaft tower, drop to the far right floor alcove 
    behind a breakable statue.

• Urn #5:
  - Location: Stage 4 (Catacombs Lower Cavern)
  - Details: Break open a stone urn situated behind a wall of spikes (requires 
    Ground Gargoyle or Claw Shot).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. VELLUMS / MAGIC SCROLLS (5 TOTAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Vellum #1:
  - Location: Stage 1 (Graveyard)
  - Details: Headbutt the small hippogriff statue sitting on a high platform 
    near the large background gargoyle statue.

• Vellum #2:
  - Location: Stage 2 (Town Catacombs Entrance)
  - Details: Break open the wall next to the pot shop using the Buster Shot.

• Vellum #3:
  - Location: Stage 5 (Sky Towers)
  - Details: In the vertical windy room, climb to the top-right corner to find 
    the scroll sitting on a high ledge.

• Vellum #4:
  - Location: Stage 4 (Underground River)
  - Details: Destroy a submerged urn located at the far right of the underwater 
    passageway (requires Tidal Gargoyle).

• Vellum #5:
  - Location: Stage 5 (Mountain Pass 2)
  - Details: Destroy the last skull pile sitting on a floating platform right 
    before the boss fight with Grewon.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TALISMANS (5 TOTAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Crown Talisman (Increases Coin Drop Rate):
  - Location: Stage 5 (Sky Towers)
  - Details: Drop to the bottom-left door in the windy tower section. Headbutt 
    the 3rd (farthest left) mini statue.

• Skull Talisman (Increases Health Drop Rate):
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest)
  - Details: Use Buster to break the stone blocks sealing a door frame in the 
    burning area, then headbutt to enter the cave.

• Armor Talisman (Halves Incoming Damage):
  - Location: Stage 5 (Mountain Peak - Ice Cave)
  - Details: Climb up the ice shaft to find a frozen skeleton in an alcove. 
    Smash it using the Ground Gargoyle.

• Fang Talisman (Increases Attack Power):
  - Location: Stage 4 (Catacombs)
  - Details: Fall through a false pit floor near the end of the catacombs section 
    to enter a hidden cavern containing the talisman.

• Hand Talisman (Speeds Up Fireband's Charge Shots):
  - Location: Stage 2 (Forest Lake / Submerged Area)
  - Details: Swim to the far right underwater wall using Tidal Gargoyle and 
    break open the hidden alcove.
