                       DEMON'S CREST (SNES)
              CRESTS & CREST FRAGMENTS ABILITIES GUIDE

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE FIVE MAIN CRESTS (FORM TRANSFORMATIONS)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ CREST OF FIRE (Base Form / Transformations) ]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The central crest of the game. Restoring its five crest fragments restores 
Firebrand to his full natural powers and unlocks enhanced elemental fire shots.

• Default Abilities:
  - Hovering horizontally in mid-air (press Jump while in air).
  - Headbutt / Wall-climb using claws (clinging to walls).
  - Shoots standard Fire, Buster, Tornado, Claw, or Dragon Fire shots (selected
    in menu once acquired).

• Destructible Obstacles:
  - Standard Fire: Torches/lanterns (lighting rooms or revealing hidden paths).
  - Buster Shot: Cracked stone blocks, weak masonry walls, specific urns.
  - Tornado Shot: Creates temporary air platforms/whirlwinds to jump off of.
  - Claw Shot: Coats walls in resin, allowing Firebrand to climb spikes/damaging
    surfaces.
  - Dragon Fire: Highest damage projectile; incinerates tough obstacles and
    bosses faster.

• Notable Level Applications:
  - Stage 1 (Colosseum / Graveyard): Use Buster to break stone barriers.
  - Stage 2 (Town / Forest): Use Tornado to reach high ledges; Buster to break
    hidden background walls.
  - Stage 3 (Cathedral / Caves): Use Claw to climb spiked shaft walls.
  - Stage 4 (Underground Catacombs): Use Fire to light dark passages.


[ CREST OF EARTH (Ground Gargoyle) ]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transforms Firebrand into a heavy, four-legged armored gargoyle.

• Form Abilities:
  - Ground Charge / Dash attack (breaks through obstacles and damages enemies).
  - Cannot fly or hover (wings are replaced by heavy armor).
  - Reduced jump height, but high defense and heavy impact.
  - Shoots the "Sonic Wave" / Ground Blade projectile along the floor.

• Destructible Obstacles:
  - Heavy rock piles, dense stone walls, and specific ground boulders that 
    impede progress.

• Notable Level Applications:
  - Stage 1 (Graveyard / Forest transition): Charge through thick stone blocks.
  - Stage 2 (Forest): Clear large stone barricades along the forest floor.
  - Stage 4 (Catacombs): Smash heavy statues blocking progression routes.


[ CREST OF AIR (Aerial Gargoyle) ]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transforms Firebrand into a winged, harpylike creature designed for vertical 
and free movement.

• Form Abilities:
  - True 8-directional free flight (ascend, descend, and fly freely).
  - Increased speed in mid-air.
  - Shoots the "Air Cutter" projectile (a sharp blade of wind).

• Destructible Obstacles:
  - Vines, ropes, thorns, and light hanging obstacles blocking ceiling paths.

• Notable Level Applications:
  - Stage 2 (Forest Treetops): Ascend to top branches to find secret items.
  - Stage 3 (Caves / Towers): Fly upward through tall vertical shafts without 
    needing walls.
  - Stage 5 (Sky / Mountain Towers): Essential for navigating open air chasms 
    and strong wind gusts.


[ CREST OF WATER (Ocean Gargoyle) ]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transforms Firebrand into a aquatic gargoyle suited for underwater exploration.

• Form Abilities:
  - Infinite swimming maneuverability underwater.
  - Unaffected by water currents that push normal forms back.
  - Can dive deep underwater without taking continuous environmental damage.
  - Shoots the "Stream" projectile (high-velocity water blast).

• Destructible Obstacles:
  - Underwater rock barriers, submerged coral walls, underwater urns.

• Notable Level Applications:
  - Stage 3 (Flooded Caves / Sunken Ruins): Navigate underwater tunnels and 
    reach submerged chests/tanks.
  - Stage 4 (Underground Waterways): Swim against strong currents to reach 
    hidden spell urns and vellum.


[ CREST OF TIME (Legendary Gargoyle) ]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transforms Firebrand into a golden, ancient warrior form representing maximum 
physical power.

• Form Abilities:
  - Cuts all incoming damage in half (doubles effective health).
  - Enhanced mobility: hover capability combined with faster movement speed.
  - Fires a high-powered energy beam capable of damaging all enemy types.

• Destructible Obstacles:
  - Can destroy all standard Buster-type stone blocks and barriers with basic 
    attacks.

• Notable Level Applications:
  - Late-game stages and Boss Rematches: Essential for surviving intense boss 
    fights (e.g., Phalanx, Dark Demon).
  - Used in end-game stages to easily clear through enemy-dense corridors.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CREST OF HEAVEN (ULTIMATE FORM)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ CREST OF HEAVEN (Ultimate Gargoyle) ]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Acquired by collecting all Crests, Crest Fragments, Health Tanks, Spell Urns, 
and Talismans. Unlocks Firebrand's ultimate form.

• Form Abilities:
  - Combines ALL powers into one form: Free flight (Air), heavy dash (Earth), 
    underwater swimming (Water), maximum defense (Time), and charge shots.
  - Charges up to release a devastating screen-clearing energy blast.

• Destructible Obstacles:
  - Destroys all breakable blocks, walls, vines, and underwater barriers.

• Notable Level Applications:
  - Final Secret Boss Fight: Required to reasonably combat and defeat the 
    true final boss, the Dark Demon.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CREST FRAGMENTS (FIRE CREST SHOTS)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Fire (Default):
  - Base flame shot. Ignites torches in dark levels (Stage 4 Catacombs).

• Buster:
  - Destroys cracked stone blocks and walls. Opens shortcut doors across 
    Stages 1, 2, and 3.

• Tornado:
  - Creates air whirlwinds. Used in Stage 2 (Town/Forest) and Stage 5 (Towers) 
    to reach high ledges without Aerial Gargoyle.

• Claw:
  - Sticks to walls/spikes. Used in Stage 3 (Cathedral) to climb spiked walls.

• Dragon:
  - Pure offensive power. Destroys tough barriers and inflicts maximum fire 
    damage on bosses.
