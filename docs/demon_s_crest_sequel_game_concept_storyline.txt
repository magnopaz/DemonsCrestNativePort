================================================================================
           DEMON'S CREST: REIGN OF THE RED BLAZE (FAN GAME CONCEPT)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OVERVIEW & PREMISE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title: Demon's Crest II: Age of the Emperor
Genre: Action-Platformer / Metroidvania
Setting: The Ghoul Realm (Demon Realm)
Protagonist: Firebrand (The Legendary Red Blaze)

Synopsis:
Following the catastrophic battles of 'Demon's Crest', Firebrand defeated the 
usurper Phalanx and sealed away the dangerous Crest of Heaven. With the power 
vacuum left in the Ghoul Realm, ancient dark overlords—the high-ranking demon 
emperors from 'Super Ghouls 'n Ghosts'—have descended from the Nether Depths to 
claim the shattered realm for themselves. 

Led by Emperor Sardius, these ancient warlords view Firebrand not as a hero, 
but as an arrogant pest who ruined the hierarchy of the demon world. To truly 
become the undisputed Ruler of the Ghoul Realm, Firebrand must embark on a 
brutal crusade against Sardius's dread generals, absorbing their dark essence 
and reclaiming his throne as the legendary Red Blaze.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MAIN CHARACTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* FIREBRAND (The Red Blaze):
  The legendary gargoyle warrior. Ambitious, ruthless, and loyal to no one. He 
  seeks total domination over the Ghoul Realm, rejecting both human salvation 
  and demon subservience.

* EMPEROR SARDIUS (The Ultimate Overlord):
  The massive, golden-armored emperor of dark realm conquest. He views the 
  surface world (Arthur's realm) and the Ghoul Realm as his birthright. He 
  despises Firebrand for causing chaos among the demon factions.

* ASTAROTH & NEBIROTH (The Twin Crown Dukes):
  High-ranking demonic nobility who command the infernal legions. They serve 
  as Sardius's chief strategists and warlords.

* THE DARK MAIDEN (Ariel):
  A mysterious demon sorceress who acts as an ambiguous mentor/guide to 
  Firebrand, nudging him toward conflict with Sardius for her own inscrutable 
  reasons.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. NARRATIVE STRUCTURE & ACT BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROLOGUE: ASHES OF THE CRESTS
-----------------------------
After his victory over Phalanx, Firebrand rests atop the Obsidian Spire, holding 
the elemental Crests (Fire, Earth, Air, Water, and Time). Suddenly, the sky of 
the Ghoul Realm bleeds crimson as a massive dark portal opens in the stratosphere. 
Emperor Sardius launches a surprise orbital strike from his flying fortress, 
the Imperial Citadel. 

Sardius's vanguard—led by the resurrected Lord Astaroth—shatters Firebrand's 
elemental crests into raw "Prism Shards" and scatters them across the six 
domains of the realm. Firebrand is left gravely wounded and stripped of his 
transformation forms. Driven by pure fury, Firebrand swears to slay Sardius and 
slaughter every general under his command.

---

ACT I: THE FALLEN DOMAINS
-------------------------
Firebrand must travel across the fractured Ghoul Realm to hunt down Sardius's 
generals, who are using the Crest Shards to empower their own bio-monstrosities.

1. Area 1: The Sunken Graveyard of Ships (Coastal Ruins)
   * Context: The aquatic territory once guarded by Phalanx's minions, now 
     overrun by giant sea leviathans.
   * Boss: Cockatrice (The Mutated Twin-Headed Serpent from Super Ghouls 'n Ghosts).
   * Story: The Cockatrice guards the Crest Shard of Water. It attempts to petrify 
     Firebrand, but Firebrand tears through its dual necks, reclaiming the Water 
     Form (allowing deep underwater movement and ice-blade attacks).

2. Area 2: The Rotting Canopy (The Phantom Forest)
   * Context: A decaying forest where spirit trees and cursed apparitions linger.
   * Boss: Wood Spirit / Barnacle Terror (Gould / Forest Demon).
   * Story: Firebrand navigates toxic spores and phantom traps to locate the 
     Earth Shard. Upon defeating the ancient forest monstrosity, he unlocks the 
     Ground Form (rebuilding his brute strength and headbutt destruction capabilities).

---

ACT II: WAR OF THE CORONATION
-----------------------------
With two Crests partially restored, Firebrand's return spreads terror throughout 
Sardius's exotics units. Sardius dispatches his highest generals to eliminate 
the Red Blaze before he reaches the Citadel.

3. Area 3: The Magma Caldera (Infernal Furnace)
   * Context: A subterranean volcano where Sardius's war machines and demonic 
     armor are forged.
   * Boss: Flame Dragon (Gildaran).
   * Story: Firebrand engages in an aerial chase across rising lava. Defeating 
     Gildaran restores the Fire Form (boosting his fiery breath weapons and flight 
     speed).

4. Area 4: The Tower of Whispers (Storm Peaks)
   * Context: A floating spire buffeted by perpetual hurricanes, serving as the 
     communication hub for Sardius's fleet.
   * Boss: Freya / Storm Weaver (The Cloud Sorceress).
   * Story: Firebrand scales the tempestuous spire, slaying the sorceress to 
     reclaim the Air Form (unlocking infinite vertical flight and gale-force blasts).

---

ACT III: THE HIGH COMMAND
-------------------------
Having restored his core transformations, Firebrand invades Sardius's vanguard 
strangleholds on the gateway to the upper atmosphere.

5. Area 5: The Dread Citadel Gatehouse
   * Context: The outer defenses of Sardius's floating empire.
   * Boss: Nebiroth (The Tyrant Duke).
   * Story: Nebiroth taunts Firebrand, claiming that gargoyles are mere hounds 
     built to serve emperors. Firebrand disembowels Nebiroth in a visceral showdown. 
     Nebiroth's dying breath warns that Astaroth awaits in the inner sanctum.

6. Area 6: The Chamber of Illusions
   * Context: A twisted hall of mirrors inside the Citadel.
   * Boss: Astaroth (Reborn in Dual Form).
   * Story: Astaroth unleashes devastating laser attacks and fire magic. Mid-fight, 
     he morphs into his true form (Nebiroth/Astaroth hybrid demon). Firebrand 
     uses switching Crest mechanics to counter his elemental shields, breaking 
     Astaroth permanently.

---

ACT IV: EMPEROR OF THE UNDERWORLD
----------------------------------
7. Final Area: The Golden Throne of Sardius
   * Context: The apex of the Imperial Citadel hovering above the entire Demon Realm.

   * Phase 1 (Sardius in Armor):
     Sardius fights in his massive golden armor, firing laser beams from his 
     crown and summoning energy platforms. Firebrand must maneuver vertically 
     using Air Form and strike his exposed chest core using Ground and Fire Forms.

   * Phase 2 (Sardius - Unbound Astral Entity):
     Upon shattering the golden suit, Sardius transforms into a towering cosmic 
     demon of raw dark magic. 

   * Climax / Secret Mechanics:
     If the player collected all hidden Artifacts and secret crest runes throughout 
     the game, Firebrand unlocks the ultimate transformation: THE OVERLORD FORM 
     (Ultimate Red Blaze). With crimson wings, gold-tinted horns, and apocalyptic 
     firepower, Firebrand crushes Sardius's dark core once and for all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ENDING PATHS (MULTIPLE ENDINGS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* BAD ENDING (Missing Crest Shards):
  Firebrand defeats Sardius but succumbs to his wounds. Without the complete 
  Crests, the Ghoul Realm falls into total chaos and ruin, leaving it vulnerable 
  to human knight invasions (Arthur's realm).

* NORMAL ENDING (All Crests Found, No Secret Artifacts):
  Firebrand slays Sardius and claims the Golden Throne. The remaining demon 
  hordes bow before the Red Blaze. Firebrand sets his sights on expanding his 
  empire across the dimensional rift into the human world (setting the stage 
  for the events of 'Ghosts 'n Goblins').

* TRUE / SECRET ENDING (100% Completion + Overlord Form):
  Firebrand utterly destroys Sardius and absorbs his cosmic essence. He rejects 
  the concept of thrones or kingdoms. Instead, he shatters the dimensional 
  barriers entirely, uniting the Ghoul Realm and Human Realm into a single dark 
  playground under his sole, eternal dominion. A silhouette of Arthur looking 
  at a blood-red sky appears in the final frame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GAMEPLAY & SYSTEM HIGHLIGHTS FOR DEVELOPMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Crest Fusion System:
   Allows combining elements (e.g., Fire + Air = Inferno Jet; Ground + Water = 
   Acid Mud Burst) to solve environmental puzzles and deal bonus damage to 
   specific bosses from Super Ghouls 'n Ghosts.

2. Boss Rematches & Lore Relics:
   Defeating bosses drops "Demon Lore Tablets" that detail the history of the 
   war between Arthur's Kingdom, Sardius's Empire, and the Red Arremer Tribe.

3. Soundtrack Style:
   Gothic orchestral mixed with 16-bit SNES soundchip synthesis—combining 
   the dark organ melodies of 'Demon's Crest' with the brass fanfares of 
   'Super Ghouls 'n Ghosts'.
